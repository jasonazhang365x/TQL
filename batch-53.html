A new category: treating plants that ship utility poles, crossarms, piling and crane-mat timbers. Every new substation tap, distribution rebuild and data center service line needs poles, and these plants ship by the truckload to utilities and line contractors. From the NRECA approved-plant list, Koppers plant pages and a treating plant directory. Phones are each plant's own line. None were in earlier files. Each company name links to a Google search of the name and address.

Load notes Long transmission poles run over-length and need a pole trailer and permits; quote distribution poles (up to about 50 ft) and crossarms, which are legal on a flatbed. Koppers plants are one company; each plant books its own outbound loads but ask about a central freight team.
