First batch under the new equipment rule: machines that have to move to every data center and grid job. These are the makers and importers of aerial lifts, telehandlers, excavators, loaders and cranes, listed once each at the plant or US headquarters that ships finished machines to dealers and rental fleets. Contacts come from the ForConstructionPros company directory or the maker's own locations page. None were in earlier files. Each company name links to a Google search of the name and address.

Load notes Machines move on flatbeds and step decks; big excavators and cranes need RGN trailers and oversize permits. The importers (Takeuchi, SANY, HD Hyundai, LiuGong, Kobelco, Hitachi, Tadano, Liebherr) bring machines in through Savannah, Brunswick, Houston or Norfolk and truck them to dealers. Several are big multi-plant groups (JLG/Oshkosh, Genie/Terex, Volvo, CASE/CNH, Kubota, Manitowoc), so ask who books outbound loads. HD Hyundai merged its Hyundai and Develon equipment units, so one office may now handle both. Some directory listings are a few years old; confirm the number on the first call.
