Dealers and rental fleets that move machines to data center and grid jobs. Rental companies shift equipment between branches and out to large jobsites, and Cat dealers move new, used and rental machines and generators across their territories. Each is listed once, at the headquarters or main store. The Cat dealers chosen cover the main data center states (Virginia, Georgia, Arizona, Texas, Ohio, Indiana, Pennsylvania, Louisiana, the Carolinas, Tennessee, Mississippi, Kentucky, New Jersey, Missouri, Iowa, Oklahoma and the Dakotas). None were in earlier files as leads. Each company name links to a Google search of the name and address.

Load notes Dealers and rental companies run their own lowboys and delivery trucks for local moves, so expect them to tender longer moves and overflow rather than every load. Sunbelt and Sunstate have published fleet evidence and are tagged. HOLT CAT also sells an equipment hauling service. Carter, Empire, Gregory Poole, Louisiana CAT and Mustang CAT show only toll-free numbers or no number in the sources found; use the store line from their websites. Fabick, Altorfer, Boyd, Puckett, Blanchard, Foley and Ohio CAT are listed at their main rental store page, which may not be the head office.
