Mat makers and timber mills that ship crane mats and access mats, which go to almost every transmission line, substation and large data center site to carry cranes and trucks over soft ground. Most come from the NC Forest Service mat supplier list (revised April 2026), plus the three large composite and laminated mat makers. None were in earlier files. Each company name links to a Google search of the name and address.

Load notes NPK International (formerly Newpark) and Sterling rent mats as well as sell them, and NPK runs install crews, so expect some moves on their own trucks. Royal Oak is part of a larger charcoal company; it is listed at the Brandsville mill because that is the mat shipper named on the state list. Fuqua Sawmill's phone has a South Florida area code on the state list, so confirm it. Bridgewell is a wholesaler and Checkers mats ship palletized, so those two can move as LTL too.
